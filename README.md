# Adidas Interactive Sales Dashboard

An interactive data visualization dashboard offering key operational insights into Adidas sales metrics, regional distribution, product category performances, and quarterly trends across five key markets in the Middle East region.

## 📌 High-Level Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Units Sold:** 3,550
* **Total Profit:** 85,070
* **Active Regions Covered:** 5 (Egypt, Iraq, KSA, Lebanon, Oman)

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Breakdown

### 1. Profit Per Product Line & Category
* **Top Performing Category:** Footwear Men (29,296) followed by Footwear Women (22,836).
* **Other Key Categories:** Apparel Men (11,960), Apparel Women (9,683), Accessories Men (5,465), Accessories Women (4,661).
* **Low Volume Categories:** Footwear Other (695), Apparel Other (414), Accessories Other (59).

### 2. Sales Method Distribution
* **Online Sales:** 46% (Dominant sales channel)
* **Retail Sales:** 36%
* **Outlet Sales:** 12%
* **Wholesale Sales:** 6%

### 3. Individual Product Profitability
* **Top Product:** Predator Freak (9,218)
* **High Performers:** Ultraboost Light (8,876), Copa Sense (8,087), Primegreen Jacket (7,687), Ultraboost 22 (7,681), Z.N.E. Hoodie (7,070)
* **Core Products:** NMD_R1 (5,833), Superstar (5,728), Stan Smith (5,662)
* **Entry Level:** Originals Backpack (3,355)

### 4. Sales Trend By Quarters (2023 – 2025)
* **Peak Quarter:** Q4 2023 at 31,322 units/sales.
* **Lowest Point:** Q3 2024 at 22,498 units/sales.
* **Current Trajectory:** Consistent recovery through 2025 (Q1: 24,701 | Q2: 26,518 | Q3: 25,580).

### 5. Regional Sales Performance
* 🥇 **Egypt:** 86,028 *(Market Leader)*
* 🥈 **Iraq:** 81,725
* 🥉 **Oman:** 69,057
* 4️⃣ **KSA:** 32,966
* 5️⃣ **Lebanon:** 17,602
